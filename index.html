The Route

Zürich to Paris, by boat where it counts. The bold line is the Viking Freya, river-borne from Basel to Trier; the dashed segments are overland — the pre-cruise transfer to Basel, and the post-cruise run through Reims to Paris.

01
Sun, Nov 8 · Travel Day

In Transit

The journey begins. An overnight flight carries you from San Diego toward Switzerland — the first leg of jet lag recovery starts in the air.

Highlights

  • SAN → DTW → AMS → ZRH, arriving the next morning
  • Economy class, all segments (Delta / KLM)
  • Overnight transatlantic leg — try to sleep as much as possible
  • Connection times: DTW 2h33m, AMS 3h40m outbound

Tips & Notes

  • Pack comfortable layers, a neck pillow, an eye mask, and melatonin if you use it.
  • Bring light snacks in case airline meals don't agree with an odd sleep schedule.
  • Set your watch to Zürich time as soon as you board.
Flight Details: SAN→DTW DL 754 (6:15A–1:42P) · DTW→AMS DL 132 (4:15P–6:00A+1, overnight) · AMS→ZRH DL 9437 (9:40A–11:00A, operated by KLM)
02
Mon, Nov 9 · Switzerland

Zürich, Switzerland

Touchdown in Switzerland. A gentle first day is the whole point — walk a little, see the lake, and let your body clock start resetting before the busier days ahead.

Highlights

  • Arrive Zürich Airport (ZRH), transfer to Renaissance Zürich Tower Hotel
  • Light walking in Zürich's Old Town (Altstadt) in the afternoon
  • Stroll Bahnhofstrasse, Zürich's grand shopping street
  • Lake Zürich waterfront at sunset
  • Easy Swiss dinner nearby, early bedtime

Tips & Notes

  • Resist a long nap — push through until at least 8 PM local time.
  • Switzerland uses Swiss Francs (CHF), not euros.
  • Hotel: Renaissance Zürich Tower Hotel, Turbinenstrasse 20, 8005 Zürich.
03
Tue, Nov 10 · Switzerland

Zürich & Lucerne

An included morning on Lake Zürich, then the well-rested version of the Swiss Alps day trip — Lucerne's Chapel Bridge and Old Town, an hour away by train.

Highlights

  • Scenic boat cruise on Lake Zürich with alpine views
  • Rathaus (1690), Fraumünster (Chagall windows), Großmünster
  • Afternoon: train to Lucerne — Chapel Bridge (1333), Lion Monument
  • Old Town Lucerne & Musegg Wall towers (free to climb)
  • Lakeside lunch, return to Zürich in the evening
Reserved · Included

05
Thu, Nov 12 · Rhine Port

Strasbourg, France

Alsace's storybook capital, where French and German culture blend into canals, timbered houses, and one of Europe's tallest Gothic cathedrals.

Highlights

  • Petite France — the former Tanners' District, canals and timbered houses
  • Strasbourg Cathedral — world's tallest building from 1647 to 1874
  • Astronomical clock performance at solar noon (~12:30 PM)
  • Place Kléber, the city's main square
  • Alsatian lunch: tarte flambée (flammekueche)
Reserved · Included

  • Look up at the cathedral façade — the stone carving detail is extraordinary.
  • If Christmas markets are open, Place Kléber is the main spot.
06
Fri, Nov 13 · Rhine Port

Speyer, Germany

A lighter day in a town holding one of the largest Romanesque cathedrals in the world — a UNESCO World Heritage Site with eight emperors entombed inside.

Highlights

  • Speyer Cathedral (Kaiserdom) — UNESCO World Heritage Site
  • Crypts of Holy Roman Emperors beneath the cathedral
  • Altpörtel (Old Gate) — last remains of the town fortifications
  • Maximilianstrasse, the main pedestrian street

Sat, Nov 14 · Rhine Port

Mainz, Germany

Gutenberg's city. A short morning tour covers 2,000 years of history, then the whole afternoon is free to rest or wander the Old Town.

Highlights

  • Mainz Cathedral (St. Martin's) — over 1,000 years old
  • Gutenberg Museum — original Gutenberg Bibles, printing history
  • Chagall windows at St. Stephan's Church
  • Old Town squares and fountains

Middle Rhine, Koblenz & Winningen

The most scenic stretch of the whole cruise: a morning of castles gliding past on the Middle Rhine, then an afternoon where the Rhine meets the Moselle.

Highlights

  • Lorelei Rock, Marksburg Castle, Pfalzgrafenstein
  • Deutsches Eck — confluence of the Rhine and Moselle
  • Ehrenbreitstein Fortress (cable car), Koblenz Old Town
  • Winningen — small Moselle wine village

Mon, Nov 16 · Moselle

Cochem, Germany

A fairytale Moselle village dominated by a hilltop castle, surrounded by some of the steepest vineyard terraces in the wine world.

Highlights

  • Reichsburg Castle (Cochem Castle) — 1,000 years old, Gothic Revival restoration
  • Old Town cobblestone streets, 15th-century St. Martin's Church
  • Moselle wine country drive & local winery visit
  • Riesling tasting — the Moselle's world-renowned specialty

Tue, Nov 17 · Moselle

Bernkastel & Trier

A free morning to wander Bernkastel's medieval Markt Square on your own, then an easy afternoon in Trier, Germany's oldest city.

Highlights

  • Bernkastel Markt Square — one of Germany's most beautiful medieval squares
  • Spitzhäuschen — the leaning house from 1416
  • Porta Nigra — Roman city gate, UNESCO World Heritage Site
  • Cathedral of Trier — Germany's oldest cathedral
  • Roman amphitheater and the largest Roman baths north of the Alps

Wed, Nov 18 · Transfer Day

Disembark Trier → Paris

The longest day of the trip. Disembark in Trier, visit Reims Cathedral and the Luxembourg American Cemetery, then continue by motorcoach into Paris.

Highlights

  • Luxembourg American Cemetery — resting place of Gen. George S. Patton
  • Reims Cathedral — UNESCO World Heritage Site, coronation site of French kings
  • Chagall-designed stained glass windows at Reims
  • Free time in Reims — Champagne country
  • Arrive Hyatt Regency Paris Étoile by evening
Reserved · Included

  • Along the Seine to Île de la Cité and Notre-Dame's façade
  • Latin Quarter, Luxembourg Gardens, Musée d'Orsay

13
Fri, Nov 20 · Paris

Iconic Paris

The cruise package technically ends today, but you're extending through the 23rd. A full day of Paris icons, ending at Trocadéro for sunset over the Eiffel Tower.

Highlights

  • Arc de Triomphe — climb to the top (284 steps) for a panoramic view
  • Champs-Élysées, Place de la Concorde, the Egyptian Obelisk
  • Tuileries Garden
  • Trocadéro Plaza at sunset — the classic Eiffel Tower photo spot
  • Hourly sparkle show, five minutes at the top of each hour

Tips & Notes

  • Sleep in and have a late breakfast — day one of the extension should feel unhurried.
  • Sunset is around 5:00 PM in November.
  • Café de l'Homme on the Trocadéro side has a stunning tower view for dinner.
Confirmed: Return flights (DL 229 / DL 369) are booked for Nov 23, matching the full Paris extension.
14
Sat, Nov 21 · Paris

Historic Paris

Notre-Dame, Sainte-Chapelle, and the Marais — a day for the island at the heart of Paris, then an evening cruise on the Seine.

Highlights

  • Notre-Dame Cathedral — reopened December 2024 after restoration
  • Sainte-Chapelle — fifteen stained-glass windows in a single small chapel
  • Île Saint-Louis, Berthillon ice cream
  • The Marais — Place des Vosges, Paris's oldest square (1612)
  • Evening Seine River cruise, about an hour, from Pont Neuf

Tips & Notes

  • Book free timed-entry slots for Notre-Dame in advance at notredamedeparis.fr.
  • Sainte-Chapelle entry is about €13; the Seine cruise about €16.
Still Deciding: Chartres Cathedral, about an hour outside Paris, remains under consideration as a day trip in place of the plan above — it would preserve tomorrow's Sacré-Cœur Mass either way.
15
Sun, Nov 22 · Paris

Sacred Paris

Montmartre and its basilica, the Miraculous Medal Chapel, and a farewell dinner with Rudy and Trining back at the Hyatt.

Highlights

  • Sacré-Cœur Basilica — free entry, optional dome climb (~€8)
  • 11:00 AM Solemn Mass at Sacré-Cœur with the famous choir
  • Montmartre — Place du Tertre, Café des Deux Moulins (Amélie)
  • Chapel of Our Lady of the Miraculous Medal, 140 Rue du Bac
  • Evening: farewell dinner with Rudy & Trining at the Hyatt

Tips & Notes

  • Take the funicular up to Sacré-Cœur for €2 if the stairs feel like a lot.
  • La Maison Rose in Montmartre is a lovely, historic lunch stop.
  • Rudy & Trining return from Mont Saint-Michel that afternoon — plan the dinner meetup accordingly.
16
Mon, Nov 23 · Travel Day

Paris → San Diego

Final breakfast at the Hyatt, then the long trip home — a quiet close to an unhurried trip.

Highlights

  • Transfer to Charles de Gaulle Airport (CDG)
  • Depart Paris (CDG) → San Diego (SAN), Economy
  • Arrive San Diego International Airport

Tips & Notes

  • Allow plenty of time at CDG — arrive 3 hours before an international flight.
  • Taxi to CDG runs about €55, or use the arranged transfer.
